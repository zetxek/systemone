This is a standalone page — no dates, tags, or author card, per the design
spec's standalone-page recipe. It exists to exercise the theme's prose
typography at the standard `68ch` reading measure.

## Why this site exists

Systemone is built directly from a written design specification rather than
from a moodboard. Every color, size, weight, tracking value, radius, and
shadow in the theme traces back to that document, so this example site is
deliberately plain: it is here to prove the type scale, the rules, and the
geometry read correctly, not to demonstrate a finished editorial product.

## What Stage 1 covers

Stage 1 ships the token sheet, base typography and layout, and the minimal
templates needed to render a real site end to end: a home page, a section
list, single posts and standalone pages, and a 404 page.

## What comes later

Post cards, the table of contents, pagination, footnote styling, the full
code/terminal component, admonitions, tables, forms, taxonomies, shortcodes,
markdown render hooks, an image pipeline, the copy-button script, and the
home-page edge ornaments are all later-stage work.
